Access to the SurfsApp web app

The web app itself is access restricted. You can request a personal account here. After your request has been reviewed and approved, an invitation will be sent to your email address.

Your request is sent directly to SurfsApp.

Your name and email address are used to review and process the access request. Your personal access will only be created after approval.
